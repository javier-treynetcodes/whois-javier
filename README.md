## TreyNet Codes
 
TreyNet Codes is a games & media studio. Games are at the core of everything we build: every project is conceived as a complete product, with a solid technical foundation, scalable architecture and a clear focus on the experience of the people who use it.
 
Alongside games, we develop apps and software products that share the same standards: clean code, maintainable architecture and a product-first mindset.

## What I Do
 
### 💻 Software Developer
I design and build complete software products: web platforms, mobile apps and backend services. I own the whole cycle, from architecture and data model to deployment, with a focus on performance, maintainability and products that are genuinely useful.
 
### 🎮 Games Lead Developer
I lead game development at TreyNet Codes: game design, core systems, architecture and player experience. I build in Unity with C#, backed by online services for progression, data and live content.
 
### 🗂️ Therefore™ Solutions Designer
I design and implement solutions on Therefore™: document management, workflow automation and integration with existing business systems. I turn company processes into structured, traceable and efficient digital workflows.
